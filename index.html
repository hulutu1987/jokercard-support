jokecard:king power support

Tap the book on the table for hand rankings and controls. Tap up to five cards to select them. The play button previews their total score. Exchange replaces the selected cards and uses one exchange.

The sparkle button opens the effect studio. Preview either finish before buying. If a purchase is pending, reopen the studio after approval. For directly purchased finishes, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used to buy them. Token balances and finishes redeemed with tokens are stored on your device.

Your current act saves after each move. Rotate the phone at any time. On iPhone Duo, fold across the screen to separate the scoreboard from the hand, then open flat to return to the regular table.

To open a pack, tap the sparkle button, choose Card packs & collection, then swipe across the dotted seal. Tap each of the five cards to reveal it. In Collection, select a design to refine duplicates or craft its golden finish.
