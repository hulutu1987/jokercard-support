Game support

Play and score

Choose up to five cards. Tap Play to score the hand shown in the preview, or Exchange to replace your selection. The book on the table lists hand patterns and scoring rules. Rotate the phone for a side-by-side layout. On iPhone Duo, a supported tabletop posture separates the score panel from your hand.

Blinds, decks and unlocks

In the expanded game, a run has eight antes. Each ante has a Small, Big and Boss blind. Reach the chip target before you run out of plays. The table shows the current boss rule.

Tap the stack of cards to open the Deck library. Each deck lists its starting effect and any unlock condition. New decks and jokers can unlock through milestones such as scoring a particular hand or clearing a blind. Starting another run replaces your current run and keeps your unlock progress and collection on this device.

Use the blind shop

Clear a blind and tap Continue to spend earned run coins. Hire up to four troupe jokers, buy skills, train a hand, or add a copy of a played card to the run deck. Hired jokers resolve from left to right. Tap a hired joker at the table to move it earlier or later. A multiplier that doubles the score can have a different result depending on its position.

You can carry two consumables. Their descriptions explain any selection required before use. Charts improve a hand pattern. Other consumables can change cards or affect the current blind. Choose Bank coins and continue when you are ready for the next blind.

Buy and restore cosmetic items

The sparkle button opens the Effect studio. You can preview Solar Flare and Lunar Halo there. If an Apple purchase is pending, reopen the studio after approval. To restore a directly purchased finish, use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used to buy it. Your token balance and finishes redeemed with tokens are saved on this device.

Open collector packs

In the Effect studio, choose Card packs & collection. Claim the free starter pack or spend 25 tokens on another pack. Swipe across the dotted seal, then tap each of the five cards to reveal it. Each draw independently selects one of 54 equally likely designs, with a 10% chance of a gold finish. In Collection, you can refine duplicates into dust and craft a golden finish.
