Game support · Privacy

Privacy policy

Game progress

jokercard stores gameplay data locally on your device. This includes your current run, starting deck, hand and blind progress, run coins, skill and joker lineup, consumables, modified cards, and the records used to unlock decks and jokers. The game also stores your collection, dust, token balance, pack-opening progress and redeemed finishes locally.

You can play without creating a game account. The game has no advertising SDK, third-party analytics or developer-operated gameplay server.

Purchases

The Effect studio uses Apple’s StoreKit to request products, make purchases and restore eligible purchases. Apple processes payments. The game receives product and transaction information so it can deliver purchases. It keeps identifiers for delivered token transactions in local app data.

Removing the app can remove your locally saved runs, unlocks, collection, tokens and redeemed finishes. Directly purchased non-consumable finishes can be restored with the Apple Account used to buy them.
