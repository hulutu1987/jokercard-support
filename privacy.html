jokecard:king power privacy

jokercard stores your current game, token balance, unopened or partially revealed packs, collected cards and dust, redeemed finishes and identifiers for delivered token transactions in its local app data. The game has no account, advertising SDK, third-party analytics or developer-operated gameplay server.

The effect studio uses Apple's StoreKit to request products, make purchases and restore eligible purchases. Apple processes payment details. The game receives the product and transaction information it uses to deliver purchases. Removing the app can remove locally saved runs, tokens and redeemed finishes. Directly purchased non-consumable finishes can be restored with the purchasing Apple Account.
